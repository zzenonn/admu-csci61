# 10 - Digital Forensics

Slides: [10 Digital Forensics](../slides/10_digital_forensics.bento.html)
Lab: [Lab 08: The Sleuth Kit and Autopsy](../labs/lab08_sleuthkit_and_autopsy.md)

## Parts
1. Purpose: forensics and incident response, types of investigation
2. Evidence: admissible, accurate, understandable, objective; chain of custody; integrity
3. Process: NIST SP 800-86 (collect, examine, analyse, report)
4. Acquisition and integrity: order of volatility, live and dead-box, raw and E01, hashing
5. Disks: partitions, file systems, deleted files, slack, carving
6. Artefacts and timelines: MACB times, metadata, browser history
7. Anti-forensics and limits
8. Reporting: fact, inference and opinion
